Cleaner by design

Dustless demolition

Control the dust where it starts.

Fine demolition dust can travel like smoke, move through gaps and settle far beyond the work area. Source capture changes the renovation experience from the first day.

01
Captured at the toolHigh-efficiency equipment collects material close to the removal point.
02
HEPA filtrationFine particulate is filtered rather than allowed to circulate freely through the home.
03
Considerate project careA cleaner work process supports a more comfortable experience for people, pets and living spaces.

Dustless methods greatly reduce dust but do not create a completely dust-free environment. This is general information and is not medical advice.

Beneath the finish

The part you see depends on the work you do not.

Preparation, substrates, bonding and moisture management create the foundation for a finished result that performs as beautifully as it looks.

01

Appropriate bonding systems

Quality mortars such as VersaBond may be selected where appropriate for reliable adhesion and consistent performance.

02

Stable substrate preparation

Materials such as Fiberock can support durable, moisture-resistant assemblies when matched to project conditions.

03

Moisture-management planning

Systems such as RedGard or Kerdi may be used to help create a properly planned tiled shower assembly.

04

Plumbing evaluated while open

When shower walls are open, aging components can be assessed before new finishes make future access more disruptive.

Before you reach out

Good projects begin with clear expectations.

What work do you take on?

Company materials identify flooring and tile installation, bathroom remodels, kitchens and backsplashes, fireplaces, patios and decks, accent walls, pavers, dustless demolition and design consultation. Final availability and scope depend on the project, site conditions and applicable license classification.

What should I send for a proposal?

Send your name, project city or address, project type, desired outcome, approximate timing and a few current photos. That is enough to begin. Additional details can be gathered only when they are useful.

Does dustless demolition eliminate all dust?

No. It is designed to greatly reduce airborne dust by capturing material close to the source and filtering fine particulate. No demolition process can guarantee a completely dust-free environment.

Do you help with design decisions?

Design consultation is listed as an optional service. The team can discuss material direction, layout and practical considerations as part of defining the project.

How are pricing and timing determined?

Every home is different. Pricing, scope and scheduling should be confirmed after the team understands the actual space, access, substrate, selections, code considerations and other project conditions.
