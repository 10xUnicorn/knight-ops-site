How the Founder Bottleneck Index is scored

Twelve questions, two per dimension. Each answer scores 0, 4 or 8 points of founder dependency, for a maximum of 96, normalized to 100. The six dimensions are the places a founder-led company most often stalls between $5M and $50M in revenue:

  1. Decisions: how many day-to-day calls wait for you.
  2. Knowledge: how much of how the company works lives only in your head.
  3. Delivery: whether clients get the same experience when you are not on the job.
  4. Data: whether your leadership team can see the numbers without asking you or building a spreadsheet.
  5. Sales: whether revenue closes without you in the room.
  6. Systems: whether your tools talk to each other or you are the integration.

Questions founders ask

What is a founder bottleneck?+
A founder bottleneck is the point where a company's growth is limited by the founder's personal capacity because decisions, knowledge, client relationships or data only flow through them. The company has a team but still runs on one person's memory and availability.
What score is a problem?+
Under 35 means the company largely runs without you. 35 to 64 means you are the system in at least two areas. 65 and above means the company cannot operate a normal week without you, which is the most common profile at five to twenty million in revenue.
Do I have to give my email?+
No. You see your score without it. The email is optional and only used to send your breakdown and the first 90-day move for your tier.
What happens after the score?+
You get your tier, a breakdown by dimension and one recommended first move. If you want a 90-day roadmap, the next step is a complimentary Tech Discovery Call, where we decide together whether a Systems Blueprint Session is the logical next step.
