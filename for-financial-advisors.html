How to automate advisor client workflows without breaking compliance

01

Map the client journey you actually run

Prospect, discovery, proposal, onboarding, first 90 days, review cadence, referral. Write down who does each step today and where it lives.

02

Pick one system of record for client data

Your CRM becomes the source of truth. Every other tool reads from it or writes to it. Nothing lives only in a spreadsheet or an inbox.

03

Automate the triggers, not the advice

Meeting booked, account opened, paperwork returned, review due, RMD window. Each trigger fires the next task, email or checklist. The advisor still writes the advice.

04

Build the operations dashboard

Onboarding stage per household, overdue reviews, tasks by owner and revenue by segment on one screen your operations lead opens every morning.

05

Keep an audit trail on everything

Every automated message and status change is logged so a compliance reviewer can reconstruct any client interaction.

06

Deploy in layers and measure

Dashboard first, then onboarding, then reviews. Measure operations hours saved per household and days from signed to funded.
