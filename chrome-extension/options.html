Knight Ops Recorder

Connect the extension to your dashboard.

This is the KO_VIDEO_SECRET you set as a Supabase secret and a Cloudflare Worker secret. It authorises uploads. Keep it private — anyone with it can add videos to your library.

Leave blank and the extension will pull this from your dashboard on first use.

Shortcuts

  • Alt+Shift+R — start or stop recording the full screen
  • Alt+Shift+S — screenshot the current tab
  • Alt+Shift+P — pause or resume while recording
  • Right-click any page for record and screenshot options

How it works

  • Video uploads while you record, so stopping is nearly instant.
  • Deepgram transcribes it, then Claude writes the title, summary, and chapters.
  • The share link is copied to your clipboard the moment recording ends.
  • Everything lands in your library at knightops.biz/admin.
