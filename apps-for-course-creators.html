Is it better to build a custom course platform or use Teachable?

If you want full control, zero monthly fees, and a platform that matches your brand and teaching style, a custom build is the better choice. Teachable charges $39 to $199 per month, takes a percentage of sales on lower plans, and limits your design and functionality to their templates. A Knight Ops custom build is a one-time investment, and you own everything.

How fast can a custom mastermind platform be built?

Knight Ops deploys progressively rather than disappearing for months and returning with an empty handoff. Layer 1, Foundation and Business Intelligence, ships first so you can see your own numbers before anything else is scoped, and every layer after that goes live as it is finished. You get functional value throughout the build. The exact sequence is set during your Systems Blueprint Session and reordered deliberately if your constraint changes.

What features should a course creator platform include?

A strong course platform should include a member portal with login access, structured content delivery with modules and lessons, progress tracking, community or discussion features, payment processing, email automation for student engagement, a professional sales page, and analytics dashboards. Knight Ops builds all of these into a single custom platform.

Can I get a mobile app for my online course?

Yes. Native iOS and Android deployment is one of the Specialized Experiences layers of the AI Business OS and is scoped during your Systems Blueprint Session. It includes iOS and Android app deployment. Your students get a native mobile app they can download from the App Store or Google Play, with push notifications, offline access, and a seamless learning experience.
